# Allocate collateral between strategies

Your collateral is shared by Personal Trades, each strategy you run, and each strategy you copy or fade. Allocate decides how much each one can use.

1. Click **Allocate** in Portfolio, or open **Actions › Allocate**.
2. Each row shows **Reserved** (in use by open positions and orders) and **Free**.
3. Use the minus and plus controls or type an amount per row. Shortcuts: **Split evenly**, **All to Personal Trades**, **Release** on one row, **Release all**, **Reset**.
4. Click save and sign in your wallet. Moving money between strategies is a withdrawal from one and a deposit into the other.

Only free collateral can be moved. Reserved collateral stays with its positions until they close.
