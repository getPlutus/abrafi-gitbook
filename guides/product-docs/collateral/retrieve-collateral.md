# Retrieve collateral

1. Open **Actions › Retrieve collateral**. You may need to Release collateral from strategies first (step 1 of 2).
2. The panel shows **Available to retrieve**, up to what Personal Trades has free.
3. Choose which tokens to take back, or click **Proportional**. Each token shows what you retrieve and what stays.
4. Sign the transaction. The tokens move from the AbraFi contract back to your wallet.
