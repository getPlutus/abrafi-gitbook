# How much each token counts for

Each collateral token counts for a share of its value. Supply collateral and Retrieve collateral show each token's share next to it, and You can trade with already reflects it.

USDaf matters for losses. Open losses are covered by the USDaf in your collateral first. Other tokens, including sUSDaf, do not cover losses directly; losses beyond your USDaf are borrowed against them (see [How borrowing works](../risk-borrowing-liquidation/how-borrowing-works.md)).
