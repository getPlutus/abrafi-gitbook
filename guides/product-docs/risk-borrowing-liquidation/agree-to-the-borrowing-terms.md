# Agree to the borrowing terms

The first time you place a trade or start a copy or fade, AbraFi shows **Before your first copy** (or the same terms before your first trade). You agree once and it covers every later trade, copy and fade.

What the terms cover:

* Every hour, open losses your USDaf doesn't cover are borrowed in USDaf from AbraFi's debt pool against your other collateral, without a new signature. When losses shrink, the borrowed amount is returned at the next check.
* Interest runs only on what is borrowed, at a variable rate set out in the terms. Interest already charged stays.
* There is no due date. Repay in USDaf or sell collateral whenever you like.
* Your collateral is not sold while your account is healthy.
* Risks: copies and fades run without you; a fade loses when the strategy wins; past results don't predict future ones; you can lose what you put in; leverage makes gains and losses bigger.
