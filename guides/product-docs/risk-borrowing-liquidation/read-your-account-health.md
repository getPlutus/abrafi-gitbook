# Read your account health

Click the health pill at the top of any page.

* **Trading risk:** how much margin your positions need against your collateral, for example "$183 of $15,017 · Warning below $500".
* **Borrowing risk:** how much you owe against what your collateral counts for. "Nothing borrowed" when you owe nothing.
* **Positions to watch:** positions close to liquidation, for example "JUP-PERP 13% from liquidation", with the advice to watch it or reduce leverage.

Portfolio also has **How it adds up**, which breaks down your available margin. The order ticket shows how a new order would change your risk before you place it.
