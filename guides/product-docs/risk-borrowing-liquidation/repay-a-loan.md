# Repay a loan

* **Repay loan:** open **Actions › Repay loan** and repay in USDaf.
* **Sell collateral and repay:** open **Actions › Sell collateral and repay** to sell part of your collateral to cover what you owe.

Repayment covers any uncovered loss first, then interest, then principal. If open positions still need the money, the next hourly check can borrow it again. Both actions show "No loan to repay" when you owe nothing.
