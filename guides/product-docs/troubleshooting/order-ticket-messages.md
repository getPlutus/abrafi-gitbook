# Order ticket messages

* **Minimum $10:** raise the amount, or click the button to jump to the amount field.
* **Not enough margin:** lower the amount, add collateral, or allocate more to the book you're trading in.
* **Price field highlighted:** Limit and Stop orders need a price.
* **Order rejected:** a market order moved past your max slippage. Raise slippage or use a limit order.
