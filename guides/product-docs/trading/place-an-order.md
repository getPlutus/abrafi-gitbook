# Place an order

1. Go to **Trade** and pick a market.
2. Under **Opens in**, choose where the trade goes: Personal Trades (private) or one of your strategies. The tabs across the top of Trade show each book and its open positions.
3. Choose **Long** or **Short**.
4. Choose **Market**, **Limit** or **Stop**. Limit and Stop need a price.
5. Enter **You will spend** in dollars, or use 25%, 50%, 75% or Max of the available collateral.
6. Choose leverage: 1×, 2×, 3×, 5× or 10×.
7. Optional: set Take profit and Stop loss prices, Max slippage (0.1%, 0.5%, 1%) and Reduce only.
8. Review worst price, liquidation price, trading fee and **After this order**, which shows your trading risk once the order fills.
9. Submit and sign.

Minimum order is $10. If the amount is above your available margin, the button reads **Not enough margin**.
