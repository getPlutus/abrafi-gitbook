# Manage positions and orders

* **Positions** shows market, side and leverage, size, entry and mark price, margin, liquidation price, funding, P&L and status, with **Close** on each row.
* Positions opened by a copy are labelled "Copied from [strategy]" and are managed by that strategy.
* **Open orders** shows resting orders. Order states include partially filled, delayed, settling, cancelling (funds stay reserved for a few seconds), cancelled by the platform (when filling would push the account into liquidation), and rejected (for example a market order past your max slippage).
