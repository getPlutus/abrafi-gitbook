# When a market can't be traded normally

* **Off-hours:** markets with sessions, such as GOLD-PERP, show a badge outside their session and the ticket bounds the price.
* **Degraded:** fewer price sources than normal.
* **Close-only:** Reduce only is on and locked.
* **Paused:** no price and the submit button reads Trading paused.
* **No live price:** prices show as a dash and submit is off.
