# Markets

The Trade page lists perpetual markets with 24-hour change: SOL, BTC, ETH, HYPE, DOGE, XRP, AVAX, LINK, SUI, JUP, GOLD and SPY. For the selected market you see the index price, 24h change, hourly funding, time to next funding, price source, a 1H/4H/1D/1W chart, the order book and Top traders on that market.
