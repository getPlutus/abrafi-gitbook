# Deposit into a vault

Vaults pay in-kind yield on SOLaf (Solana), ETHaf and BTCaf (Ethereum). Your deposited asset grows automatically and you keep holding it as the same token.

1. On **Earn**, pick a vault, or open **Actions** and choose **Deposit/Withdraw**.
2. On the **Deposit** tab, select the vault. Each shows its 30-day APY and your current balance.
3. Enter the amount to deposit. If you need more of the token, click **Swap more**.
4. Confirm and sign.
