# Stake USDaf for sUSDaf

1. Open **Actions** and choose **Stake/Unstake**.
2. On the **Stake** tab, enter how much USDaf to stake.
3. Check **You receive** and the exchange rate. sUSDaf grows in value against USDaf, so you get fewer sUSDaf than the USDaf you stake.
4. Confirm and sign.

To unstake, use the **Unstake** tab or the **Unstake** button in Portfolio. Staked tokens can also be posted as collateral and keep earning while they back your trades ("Still earning" in Portfolio).
