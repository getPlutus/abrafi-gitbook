# Fund and trade in your strategy

1. On the strategy page, use **Add collateral** or **Allocate** to give it margin.
2. Click **Trade**. On the order ticket, check that **Opens in** shows the strategy, not Personal Trades.
3. Place trades as usual. Every fill builds the strategy's record: return, max drawdown, win rate, and the How it trades panel (markets, leverage, pace).

The strategy page's **Your trading** section is visible only to you: collateral, positions, orders, history and events.
