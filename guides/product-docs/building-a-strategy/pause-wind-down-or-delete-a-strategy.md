# Pause, wind down or delete a strategy

All of these live in the Danger zone of **Edit strategy**.

* **Pause:** no new trades and no new clients. Current clients keep their positions. Public strategies only.
* **Wind down:** no new clients or entries. Clients follow your exits until everything is closed.
* **Close all positions:** closes your positions now. For a public strategy it also pauses it. Can't be undone.
* **Delete strategy:** removes it completely. A private strategy needs no open positions. A public one can be deleted only if it never posted, never had a client and never traded.
