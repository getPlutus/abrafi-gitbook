# Make your strategy public

1. On the strategy page, open the **⋯** menu and choose **Edit strategy**.
2. Set the picture, name and description first. They are checked before anyone sees them, and they lock once the strategy is public.
3. Under **Access**, click **Make public** and confirm.
4. Optional: turn on **Allow fading** so people can take the opposite side.
5. Click **Save changes**.

Once public, the strategy shows Copy and Fade on your profile, in the Feed and on the Leaderboard, and its public record starts that day. Use **Preview as visitor** in the ⋯ menu to see what others see.
