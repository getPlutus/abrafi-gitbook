# Your first three steps

Once connected, the Trading card on Earn shows a three-step checklist:

1. **Get the tokens.** Swap what you hold (USDC, SOL, ETH, BTC) into af tokens. See [Swap into af tokens](../tokens/swap-into-af-tokens.md).
2. **Post as collateral.** Move tokens into AbraFi. They stay yours. See [Supply collateral](../collateral/supply-collateral.md).
3. **Trade.** Open your own positions or copy a strategy.

When all three are done the card changes to **You're set up to trade**, with your collateral total and links to Portfolio and Trade.
