# Find your way around

* **Feed:** posts from creators and the AbraFi Pulse platform account.
* **Earn:** vaults, liquid staking, strategies picked for the tokens you hold, and leaderboards.
* **Portfolio:** everything you own in four sections: Wallet, Earning, Collateral and Trading.
* **Trade:** markets, chart, order book and the order ticket.
* **My profile:** your record, posts, strategies, copies, trade history, analytics and the What's next checklist.
* **Actions:** one panel for every money movement: Swap/Mint, Deposit/Withdraw, Stake/Unstake, Supply collateral, Allocate, Retrieve collateral, Repay loan, Sell collateral and repay.
* **Account health pill:** shows Healthy or a warning. Click it for Trading risk, Borrowing risk and positions to watch.
* **Bell:** notifications, including risk warnings and order rejections.
* **Network fees:** SOL and ETH balances used only to pay network fees. Keep some in your wallet.
