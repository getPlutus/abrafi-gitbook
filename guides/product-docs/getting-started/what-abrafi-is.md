# What AbraFi is

AbraFi is a self-custodial app where you turn crypto into yield-bearing af tokens, earn on them, and post them as collateral to trade perpetual futures. You can trade yourself, copy or fade other traders' strategies, or build and publish your own strategy and earn from people who copy it. A social Feed ties it together: creators post their real trades, results and ideas, and readers copy or fade directly from a post. AbraFi perps are not available to US persons.
