# Key terms

* **af tokens:** AbraFi's yield-bearing tokens. USDaf (dollars), SOLaf, ETHaf and BTCaf.
* **Staked af tokens:** sUSDaf, sSOLaf, sETHaf, sBTCaf. Liquid staking tokens whose value grows against the underlying token.
* **Vault:** deposit SOLaf, ETHaf or BTCaf and keep holding the same token. Yield builds up separately and you claim it when you like. No receipt token.
* **Collateral:** tokens you move from your wallet into the AbraFi contract to back trades. They stay yours.
* **Counts for (haircut):** the share of a collateral token's value you can trade with. Each token's share is shown when you supply it.
* **Available margin:** collateral value, after haircuts, that is not already in use by positions or orders.
* **Personal Trades:** your own trades outside any strategy. Always private.
* **Strategy:** a named book of trades with a one-line pitch. Private by default; public strategies can be copied and faded.
* **Copy:** automatically take the same side as a strategy on every new trade.
* **Fade:** automatically take the opposite side of a strategy on every new trade.
* **Trading risk and Borrowing risk:** the two measures shown in Account health.
