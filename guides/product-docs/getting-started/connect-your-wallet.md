# Connect your wallet

1. Open AbraFi and click **Connect wallet** in the top right.
2. Pick a wallet: Phantom, Ledger, MetaMask, Solflare, Torus, WalletConnect or another Ethereum wallet. You can search the list. Wallets already installed in your browser show an **Installed** tag.
3. If the wallet supports more than one chain, choose **Solana** or **Ethereum** and click **Connect**.
4. Approve the connection in your wallet.

You can also use **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Apple**. By connecting you agree to the Terms and acknowledge the Privacy Policy.

Before connecting you can browse Earn (vaults, liquid staking, leaderboards) without a wallet.
