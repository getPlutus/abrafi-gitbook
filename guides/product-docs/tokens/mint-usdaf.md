# Mint USDaf

1. Open **Actions** and choose **Swap/Mint**, then the **Mint / Redeem** tab.
2. Choose USDC or USDT under **You pay** and enter an amount, or use 25%, 50%, 75% or MAX.
3. Check **You receive**. Minting is direct at 1 USDC = 1 USDaf.
4. Confirm and sign in your wallet.

Direct mint accepts USDC and USDT only. Use the same tab to redeem USDaf back to stablecoins.
