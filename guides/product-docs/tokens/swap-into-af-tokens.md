# Swap into af tokens

1. Open **Actions** and choose **Swap/Mint**, then the **Swap** tab. You can also click **Swap** next to any asset in Portfolio, which opens the swap with that asset filled in.
2. Pick what you pay (SOL, USDC, ETH, BTC) and what you receive (USDaf, SOLaf, ETHaf, BTCaf).
3. Enter an amount and review the route, rate and details.
4. Set slippage tolerance: 0.1%, 0.5%, 1%, 2.5% or custom.
5. Confirm and sign.

Portfolio flags idle assets that could be earning and tells you which af token to swap into. Assets with no af version, such as SHIB, show "Swap to an AbraFi token to earn".
