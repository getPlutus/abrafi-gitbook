# Copy a strategy

1. Click **Copy** on a strategy card, post or page. The first time, agree to the borrowing terms.
2. **Set up (1 of 2):**
   * **Direction:** Copy (same side as the creator on every new trade).
   * **Exposure ceiling:** 1× to 10×. The panel shows the creator's typical leverage and caps your copy at your ceiling.
   * **Protection:** optional take profit and stop loss as a percentage.
   * **When strategy ends:** close positions, or keep them for manual management.
   * **Current positions:** join the creator's open positions now, or start with their next trade.
3. Click **Continue to allocation**.
4. **Allocation (2 of 2):** move free collateral into the copy, from Personal Trades or other strategies. Click **Add collateral** if you need more.
5. Confirm and sign. The copy appears in **Portfolio › Copying and fading** and on your profile under Copying strategies.
