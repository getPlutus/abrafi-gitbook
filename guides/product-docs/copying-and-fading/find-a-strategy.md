# Find a strategy

* **Earn › Strategies to copy:** picks matched to the tokens you hold.
* **Leaderboards:** Best picks (chosen by AbraFi) and Most copied, with **Show all leaderboards** for other rankings.
* **Feed:** every post carries its strategy's 30-day return and Copy and Fade buttons.

Open a strategy to see its creator, pitch, return since launch versus BTC, 30-day return, max drawdown, copier count, how it trades (markets, intended leverage, declared risk out of 5, pace), what it holds now, a performance chart against BTC, win rate, audience counts, its posts, risks and a log of every trade. Audience shows counts only; who copies, fades or follows is never shown.
