# Things to know before copying

* Copies follow the creator's trades, not their leverage. After entry your leverage moves with price, so watch account health.
* A fast market can fill past your stop loss.
* If price moves too far, a copied trade can be skipped.
* Open losses your USDaf doesn't cover are borrowed every hour against your collateral.
* If a creator pauses, you keep your positions. If a creator winds down, your copy follows their exits until everything is closed.
