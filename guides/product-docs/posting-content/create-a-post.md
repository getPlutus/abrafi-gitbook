# Create a post

1. Click **Create post** (the plus in the top bar, on My profile, or on a strategy page).
2. **Choose a strategy.** Each shows its 30-day return. You can also start a new strategy here.
3. **Choose a post type** (see [Post types](post-types.md)). Types marked ✦ have a draft ready, such as a closed trade not posted yet or a scorecard for the week that just ended.
4. **Write your post:** add a caption and fill in the type's fields. The preview shows the post as it will appear in the Feed.
5. Click **Post**, or **Save draft** or **Discard**.
