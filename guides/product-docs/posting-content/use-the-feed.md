# Use the Feed

* **Tabs:** For You, Following and My activity.
* **Filters:** All, Moves, Launches, Chart takes, Results, Predictions, Scorecards, Radars, Pulse, Rebalances, Clips.
* **Act on a post:** Copy or Fade the strategy, open Discuss, or react with a quick tag such as Agree, Take profit, Too late or Great entry.
* **Alerts:** set an alert on a chart take or radar level. Alerts only notify you; a cross never fills or copies anything.
* **Predictions:** vote Yes or No. The creator's pick stays hidden until you vote if they chose that.
* **Your copy:** posts from strategies you copy show "You copy this strategy" and your matching position.
* **Verification:** unverified accounts are labelled, and claims in their posts carry "Claims like this aren't verified".
* **AbraFi Pulse:** a platform account posting market data, such as funding changes. It is labelled "From AbraFi platform data. Not advice."
