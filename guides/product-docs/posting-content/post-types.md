# Post types

| Group | Type | What it is | What you choose |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Launch | Strategy launch | Announces a strategy and pins itself to the strategy page. | Caption and cover. Markets, risk, pace and leverage come from the strategy. |
| Show a trade | Move | A real order or fill. Anything not executed reads as a plan. | The order, fill, closed trade or plan; one attachment (auto chart, chart take or clip); target and stop; whether levels are visible to everyone or copiers only. |
| Show a trade | Result | A closed trade as a P&L card, net of fees. | The closed move and a stamp: Called it, Took the L or Clean stop. |
| Show performance | Scorecard | Performance over a period against a benchmark. | Week, month or season; BTC, USDaf yield, S&P 500 or none; which stats to show. |
| Show performance | Rebalance | Allocation before and after. Every copier is notified. | Reason: member vote, signal fired, risk cut, rotation or take profit. |
| Show performance | Yield check | Realised trading P&L plus collateral yield. | Period, collateral token, and dollars or percent. |
| Share an idea | Chart take | AbraFi's chart marked up with up to 12 drawings. | Market, timeframe, and drawings: level, zone, line, arrow, label. |
| Share an idea | Radar | Up to five levels you're watching. Levels, not orders. | Markets, side and level for each. |
| Share an idea | Prediction | A price call others vote on. One vote each, no prizes. | Market; price band, yes/no or over/under; resolves Friday close, month end or a date; optional pick, which you can hide until people vote. |
| Bring content | Clip | An embedded post from X or a YouTube video. | The link, and whether it attaches to a strategy or a market. |
